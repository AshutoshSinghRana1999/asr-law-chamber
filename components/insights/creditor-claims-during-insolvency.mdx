## Introduction

The submission and verification of creditor claims is a central part of the corporate insolvency resolution process.

Creditors must ensure that their claims are supported by appropriate documentation, filed within the applicable timeline and correctly classified.

## Preparing the Claim

A creditor should ordinarily review:

- The nature and basis of the debt
- Contracts, invoices and account statements
- Security documents, where applicable
- Payment records
- Correspondence acknowledging liability
- Orders, awards or decrees supporting the claim

## Classification of Creditors

The form and treatment of a claim may depend on whether the creditor is classified as:

- A financial creditor
- An operational creditor
- A secured creditor
- An unsecured creditor
- An employee or workman
- Another stakeholder recognised under the insolvency framework

Incorrect classification can affect participation, voting rights and distribution.

## Delayed Claims

A delayed claim may create procedural complications and should be accompanied by a clear explanation and complete supporting material.

The creditor should also monitor the progress of the insolvency proceedings rather than assume that filing the claim completes the process.

## Conclusion

Careful preparation, timely filing and continued monitoring can materially affect the treatment of a creditor’s claim during insolvency proceedings.
